The Essence of Dasara

Dasara marks the triumphant climax of Navratri. On this holy day, Goddess Durga vanquished the demon Mahishasura after a fierce nine-day battle, and Bhagwan Shri Ram defeated Ravana in Lanka. It is a timeless symbol that righteousness (Dharma) will always prevail over unrighteousness (Adharma).

🍃

Exchange Shami (Jammi) Gold Leaves

In traditional custom, Jammi leaves represent gold and goodwill exchanged for health, success, and prosperity.

Iconography & Meaning

Sacred Symbols of Dasara

Each divine element carries deep spiritual energy and cosmic significance during Vijayadashami.

🔱

Trishula (Trident)

Represents Goddess Durga’s power over the three worlds (Triloka) and the destruction of negative desires, ego, and ignorance.

🏹

Bow & Arrow (Dhanush)

Symbolizes Bhagwan Shri Ram's sharp focus, truth, and the decisive destruction of evil during Ravan Dahan.

🍃

Shami / Jammi Leaves

Exchanged as symbolic gold among loved ones on Vijayadashami, signifying victory, prosperity, and lifelong peace.

🔥

Ravan Dahan Effigy

The ceremonial burning of the 10-headed effigy symbolizing the eradication of pride, lust, anger, and evil tendencies.
